A 14-year-old player struck an opponent after losing a semifinal at a Jiangsu provincial youth selection event. The association issued a public criticism, disqualified him, voided his results, and banned him from provincial events for one year.

Commentary

The case became a sportsmanship talking point on Weibo, part of a broader conversation about pressure and conduct in China's competitive youth-sports pipelines. The swift, public sanction signals governing bodies' sensitivity to reputational risk.
